Just Tangents

No Plan Just Tangents is our space to let loose and have random conversations about anything nerd-focused or pop culture-related. We're unfiltered, unapologetically ourselves, and never afraid to go off on a tangent. We try to review an anime, movie, or comic every episode, and we regularly bring on guest stars we've met through the conventions we attend. If it sounds like a group of friends hanging out and talking about the things they enjoy, that's exactly what we're going for.

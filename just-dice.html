Just Dice

No Plan Just Dice is our D&D podcast where we let loose, crack open some cold ones, roll shiny math rocks, and pretend to live in wacky, zany worlds. No overproduced, forced storytelling here—just friends sitting around a table playing the game the way we enjoy it.

Welcome to our table. Get ready for table talk, meta gaming, questionable decisions, and players doing everything they can to piss off the DM at every opportunity. Whether the plan works or ends in complete disaster, we're here for the ride.

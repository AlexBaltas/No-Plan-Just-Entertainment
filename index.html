Well, I was tasked with writing what No Plan Just Entertainment is, and to be honest, the whole premise is 4 friends being friends, talking about and doing stuff we like to do. It's grown a lot from a D&D podcast to doing live interviews, and now vending at conventions and having guest stars on. The goal is to stay us—to not be overproduced, but just 4 guys talking to strangers, meeting cool people, and selling cheap anime-inspired merch. This is literally a company made to have fun and keep us engaged in the things we love doing. If we can entertain people along the way and build a community around those same interests, even better. I can say that none of you know what's coming next with us, because even we don't!
